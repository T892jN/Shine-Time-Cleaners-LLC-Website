Privacy

How this website handles privacy, cookies, analytics, and ads.

This policy explains how visitor information may be collected and used when browsing or contacting Shine Time Cleaners.

Request availabilityCompare services

Effective date: August 2, 2026

Overview

Shine Time Cleaners respects visitor privacy. This website provides residential cleaning information, service guides, contact options, and business details. The site may use hosting logs, analytics, cookies, security tools, and advertising services such as Google AdSense if enabled.

Information collected automatically

Technical data may include IP address, browser type, device type, pages viewed, referring page, approximate location derived from IP address, and dates or times of visits. This information helps operate, secure, and improve the website.

Information visitors provide

If a visitor sends an email or uses a mailto contact form, they may provide name, email address, city, home details, service interests, scheduling preferences, and notes about pets or surfaces. This information is used to respond to the request and evaluate service availability.

Cookies and advertising

If Google AdSense or analytics tools are enabled, third parties may use cookies or similar technologies to measure traffic, personalize or limit ads where allowed, and report performance. Visitors can manage cookies through browser settings and Google advertising controls.

Sharing

Information may be shared with providers that support hosting, email, analytics, security, advertising, or business operations. Information may also be disclosed if required by law or to protect rights and safety.

Security and retention

No website can guarantee perfect security. Information is kept only as long as reasonably useful for business, legal, security, and customer-service purposes.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to contact@shinetimecleanersllc.com.
