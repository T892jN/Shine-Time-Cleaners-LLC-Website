Seasonal tips

How Missouri seasons change what your home needs cleaned.

Missouri weather can change the cleaning priorities inside a home from month to month.

Request availabilityCompare services

Spring cleaning

Spring often brings pollen, rain, mud, and open windows. Entryways, window ledges, baseboards, and floors may need extra attention. Kitchens and bathrooms also benefit from a reset after winter routines.

  • Wipe window ledges and reachable dusty trim.
  • Clean entry mats and hard floors.
  • Prioritize bathrooms where humidity begins to rise.
  • Dust bedrooms and living rooms after pollen-heavy weeks.

Summer cleaning

Summer can mean guests, kids at home, humidity, and more frequent bathroom use. Focus on kitchens, bathrooms, trash areas, and floors that feel sticky or dull.

  • Clean appliance exteriors and counters more often.
  • Keep bathroom surfaces dry and refreshed.
  • Mop entryways and kitchens after outdoor activity.
  • Rotate dusting around fans and vents when reachable.

Fall cleaning

Fall is a good time to reset before holidays. Guest rooms, dining areas, kitchens, bathrooms, and entryways often become more important.

  • Refresh guest bedrooms.
  • Deep clean bathrooms before hosting.
  • Wipe dining surfaces and chairs.
  • Clean floors before heavy indoor season.

Winter cleaning

Winter brings salt, moisture, boots, and more time indoors. Entryways and hard floors often need extra care, while dust can collect quickly in closed-up rooms.

  • Protect entry floors from wet shoes.
  • Vacuum rugs and traffic paths.
  • Dust bedrooms and living spaces.
  • Keep kitchens ready for holiday cooking.

How often should seasonal cleaning happen?

Many homes benefit from a deeper reset two to four times per year, with standard cleaning in between.

Is seasonal cleaning the same as deep cleaning?

It can be, but seasonal cleaning usually targets weather-related and hosting-related priorities.

What season is hardest on Missouri homes?

Spring and winter often create the most visible floor and entryway issues.
