Move-out cleaning guide

A practical cleaning plan for handing over keys, listing a home, or moving in.

Move-out cleaning works best after belongings are removed and utilities are still on.

Who move-out cleaning is for

Move-out cleaning is intended for renters, homeowners, landlords, property managers, and sellers who need a home cleaned after most belongings are gone. It can also help before moving into a new place if the prior condition is unknown.

The goal is not to repair damage or guarantee deposit outcomes. The goal is to clean reachable surfaces, rooms, fixtures, floors, and visible residue within an agreed scope.

  • Renters preparing for a walkthrough.
  • Homeowners listing a property.
  • Families moving into a home that needs a reset.
  • Landlords preparing a residential unit for the next occupant.

Estimated cleaning time

An apartment may take 3 to 6 hours. A house may take 5 hours to a full day or longer depending on size and condition. Empty homes can be easier to access, but heavy buildup, trash, adhesive residue, or appliance interiors can add time.

Move-out checklist

A useful move-out clean focuses on the rooms a new occupant will inspect first.

  • Kitchen: counters, sinks, faucets, cabinet exteriors, appliance exteriors, backsplash, and floors.
  • Bathrooms: toilets, tubs, showers, vanities, mirrors, fixtures, shelves, and floors.
  • Bedrooms and living areas: dusting, floors, closets when included, ledges, and touch points.
  • Entryways and halls: baseboards when included, floor edges, doors, handles, and visible marks.
  • Final pass: remove obvious dust, crumbs, and tracked-in debris from cleaned areas.

Scheduling tips

Schedule cleaning after movers have removed most belongings but before utilities are turned off. Leave access instructions, parking notes, trash rules, and any landlord requirements. If carpets are being professionally cleaned, coordinate the order so floors are not dirtied afterward.

Do you guarantee a security deposit return?

No cleaner can guarantee a landlord decision, but a thorough move-out clean can help address common walkthrough concerns.

Should the home be empty?

Empty or nearly empty is best because cleaners can reach more surfaces and floors.

Can appliance interiors be included?

They should be requested specifically because they can add significant time.
