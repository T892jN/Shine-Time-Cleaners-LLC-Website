Deep cleaning guide

A deeper reset for buildup, busy seasons, and homes that need extra attention.

Deep cleaning gives more time to the details that standard cleaning may not fully reach.

Request availabilityCompare services

Who deep cleaning is for

Deep cleaning is useful before starting recurring service, after a long gap between cleanings, before hosting, after illness, during seasonal resets, or when kitchens and bathrooms need more than a quick maintenance pass.

It is also a good choice when dust has settled on baseboards, fixtures look dull, bathroom buildup is visible, or floors need extra edge attention.

  • First-time residential cleaning customers.
  • Homes with heavy bathroom or kitchen use.
  • Households preparing for guests, events, or seasonal changes.

Estimated cleaning time

Deep cleaning often takes 4 to 8 hours or more. Large homes, multiple bathrooms, pet hair, clutter, or move-related tasks can require additional time. A realistic deep clean may need priorities: bathrooms and kitchen first, then floors, then detail dusting.

Deep cleaning checklist

A deep cleaning plan expands standard cleaning by adding more detailed attention where buildup is common.

  • Kitchen: cabinet fronts, appliance exteriors, sink detail, backsplash splashes, counter edges, and floor edges.
  • Bathrooms: fixtures, shower buildup, tub edges, toilet bases, mirror polishing, vanity fronts, and floor corners.
  • Bedrooms: lamps, nightstands, reachable decor, baseboards when included, and floor edges.
  • Living spaces: deeper dusting, fingerprints, entryway buildup, furniture edges, and high-touch surfaces.
  • Laundry and utility areas: exterior surfaces, dust-prone ledges, and hard floors when included.

What deep cleaning does not automatically include

Interior ovens, interior refrigerators, window tracks, heavy mold remediation, hoarding cleanup, pest contamination, and biohazard cleanup require separate discussion or specialized providers. Clear boundaries help keep the appointment realistic and safe.

Should I book deep cleaning before recurring service?

If the home has visible buildup, a deep first visit usually creates a better baseline for future standard cleaning.

Can deep cleaning be limited to certain rooms?

Yes. Many customers prioritize kitchens, bathrooms, or floors first.

How should I prepare?

Clear clutter from surfaces, move personal items from tubs and counters, and share any delicate-surface instructions.
