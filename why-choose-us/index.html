Why homeowners choose a cleaner

Most people do not hire a cleaner because they dislike their home. They hire help because work, family, pets, weather, errands, and moves all compete for the same time. Professional cleaning is useful when it removes friction: bathrooms are ready for guests, kitchen counters are easier to use, floors feel better underfoot, and dust does not keep creeping from one room to another.
