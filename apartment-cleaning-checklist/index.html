Apartment checklist

A compact-space checklist for apartments, condos, and townhomes.

Apartments can be smaller than houses, but high-use rooms can get dirty just as quickly.

Request availabilityCompare services

Small-space strategy

In apartments, clutter and surface buildup are more visible because rooms are closer together. Cleaning works best when counters, floors, and bathrooms are prioritized first.

  • Clear counters, tabletops, and bathroom vanities.
  • Start with kitchen and bathroom sanitation.
  • Vacuum traffic paths and rugs.
  • Mop kitchen, bathroom, and entry floors.

Apartment kitchen checklist

Apartment kitchens often have compact counters and heavy appliance use. Even a quick reset can make the whole unit feel cleaner.

  • Wipe counters, sink, faucet, backsplash, and appliance exteriors.
  • Clean cabinet fronts near handles.
  • Remove crumbs around tables and prep areas.
  • Sweep or vacuum and mop hard floors.

Apartment bathroom checklist

A single apartment bathroom can see daily use from everyone in the household, so mirrors, sinks, toilets, tubs, and floors should stay high on the list.

  • Clean mirror, vanity, sink, faucet, toilet, tub, and shower.
  • Wipe shelves and reachable ledges.
  • Remove hair and buildup from visible areas.
  • Mop floors and wipe base areas when included.

Move-out apartment notes

For move-outs, ask what the lease or property manager expects. Interior appliances, cabinet interiors, balcony areas, or carpet cleaning may require separate scheduling.

How long does apartment cleaning take?

A small apartment may take 2 to 3 hours for standard cleaning, while move-out or deep cleaning may take longer.

Can you clean before a lease inspection?

Move-out cleaning can help prepare, but lease requirements should be shared in advance.

Do apartments need deep cleaning?

Yes, especially before moving out, after a long gap, or when bathrooms and kitchens have buildup.
