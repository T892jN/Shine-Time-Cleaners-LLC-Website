Pet-friendly cleaning

Cleaning strategies for homes with dogs, cats, and everyday pet mess.

Pet-friendly cleaning is about practical household care: hair, tracked-in dirt, washable surfaces, and odor-prone spaces.

Request availabilityCompare services

Who pet-friendly cleaning is for

This service focus is for homes where pets live indoors, shed on floors or furniture-adjacent spaces, track dirt through entryways, or create extra cleaning needs around feeding areas and litter zones.

Customers should always share pet safety notes, product restrictions, allergies, and whether pets will be secured during the appointment.

  • Homes with shedding dogs or cats.
  • Apartments with litter areas or compact pet zones.
  • Families with muddy entryways or pet traffic paths.

Estimated cleaning time

Pet-heavy homes often take longer than similar homes without pets. Add time for hair removal from floor edges, repeated vacuum passes, entryway cleanup, and baseboard wiping. A small home may add 30 to 60 minutes; larger homes or heavy shedding can add more.

Pet-focused checklist

The checklist prioritizes areas where pet mess usually affects daily comfort.

  • Vacuum carpets, rugs, furniture-adjacent floors, and traffic paths.
  • Clean hard floors around doors, feeding areas, and litter zones.
  • Wipe washable baseboards, door areas, and reachable marks when included.
  • Dust surfaces where pet hair settles.
  • Discuss odor-prone rooms, product limits, and pet containment before service.

Preparation tips

Secure pets in a safe room, crate, or off-site location if needed. Pick up toys, bowls, and loose bedding unless they are part of the agreed cleaning area. Share whether a pet is nervous, reactive, likely to escape, or sensitive to sound.

Do you use pet-safe products?

Share pet needs and product restrictions before service so product choices can be discussed.

Can you clean litter boxes?

Litter box handling should be discussed separately and is not assumed in standard cleaning.

Should pets be home during cleaning?

Pets should be secured if they may be stressed, escape, or interfere with cleaning.
