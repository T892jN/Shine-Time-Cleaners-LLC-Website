Use of this website

This website provides general residential cleaning information, service descriptions, checklists, and contact options. The content is for informational purposes and does not create a service agreement by itself.

Service requests

Submitting an email or contact request does not guarantee availability, pricing, or appointment confirmation. Service details, timing, access, and scope must be reviewed and agreed before work begins.

Cleaning scope

Tasks described on this site are examples of common residential cleaning work. Final scope may vary based on home condition, safety, access, product restrictions, customer priorities, and time available.

Customer responsibilities

Customers should provide accurate home details, disclose fragile or restricted surfaces, secure valuables, provide safe access, manage pets, and identify areas that should not be cleaned.

Limitations

Shine Time Cleaners does not claim to provide restoration, remediation, pest cleanup, hazardous waste cleanup, medical cleaning, construction cleanup, or guaranteed stain removal unless separately agreed in writing.

All site content is owned by Shine Time Cleaners unless otherwise noted. Placeholder testimonials and gallery areas are clearly labeled and should be replaced only with verified customer content.
