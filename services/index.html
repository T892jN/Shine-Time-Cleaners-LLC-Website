Service overview

Residential cleaning options explained in plain language.

This page summarizes each service and links to detailed guides with checklists, time estimates, and best-fit situations.

Standard Cleaning

Routine upkeep for kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms, living areas, floors, and high-touch surfaces.

Deep Cleaning

A more detailed reset for buildup, first visits, seasonal refreshes, and homes that need extra attention.

Move-Out Cleaning

A turnover-focused clean for empty or nearly empty homes before moving in, moving out, listing, or handing over keys.

Pet-Friendly Cleaning

Practical cleaning for homes with pets, including hair, tracked-in debris, odors, and surface care.

Standard Cleaning

Standard cleaning is routine upkeep for homes that are already reasonably maintained. It typically covers kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms, living areas, dusting, vacuuming, and mopping. Estimated time is often 2 to 5 hours depending on home size and condition.

  • Kitchen counters, sinks, tables, and appliance exteriors.
  • Bathroom mirrors, sinks, tubs, showers, toilets, and floors.
  • Bedroom dusting, floor care, and light straightening.
  • Living room dusting, touch points, vacuuming, and mopping.
Read the standard cleaning guide

Deep Cleaning

Deep cleaning is for homes that need a reset. It may include more detailed attention to buildup, edges, baseboards, fixtures, reachable cabinet fronts, and high-use rooms. Estimated time is often 4 to 8+ hours.

  • Best before recurring service begins.
  • Useful after illness, hosting, renovations, or heavy use.
  • Prioritizes kitchens, bathrooms, dust buildup, and floor edges.
  • May require a larger time block than standard cleaning.
Read the deep cleaning guide

Move-Out Cleaning

Move-out cleaning helps prepare a home for turnover, listing, landlord walkthroughs, or new occupants. Estimated time can range from 4 hours to a full day depending on size, vacancy, and buildup.

  • Empty-room floor and surface cleaning.
  • Kitchen and bathroom reset work.
  • Attention to closets, cabinets, and appliance exteriors when included.
  • Best scheduled after most belongings are removed.
Read the move-out cleaning guide

Pet-Friendly Cleaning

Pet-friendly cleaning focuses on hair, paw prints, tracked-in dirt, odor-prone areas, and surfaces pets frequently touch. Estimated time depends on shedding, flooring, furniture access, and how many pet areas are included.

  • Vacuuming pet-heavy rooms and traffic paths.
  • Wiping baseboards, door areas, and washable touch points.
  • Focusing on entryways, couches nearby, and feeding zones.
  • Discussing products, allergies, and pet safety needs in advance.

Do you bring cleaning supplies?

Yes. Shine Time Cleaners brings cleaning supplies for typical residential appointments. Customers should mention delicate surfaces, product restrictions, allergies, or preferred products before service.

How long does a cleaning visit take?

Time depends on home size, condition, room count, and service type. A small maintenance visit may take a few hours, while deep cleaning or move-out cleaning can take most of a day.

Can I choose priority rooms?

Yes. Customers can prioritize kitchens, bathrooms, floors, dusting, guest areas, pet-heavy areas, or move-related details so the appointment focuses on the highest-value work first.

Do you clean apartments?

Yes. Apartments, townhomes, condos, and houses can be cleaned when the scope is residential and access details are clear.
