Standard cleaning guide

What standard cleaning includes, how long it takes, and who it helps most.

Standard cleaning is maintenance cleaning for homes that need a dependable refresh, not a full reset.

Request availabilityCompare services

Who standard cleaning is for

Standard cleaning is best for households that keep up with basic tidying but need help with recurring dust, bathroom use, kitchen mess, and floors. It suits weekly, biweekly, or monthly visits, especially when the home is not heavily cluttered and surfaces are accessible.

A standard visit is not meant to remove months of buildup or handle every hidden detail. It works best when the customer chooses priorities and the home is ready enough for cleaners to reach surfaces.

  • Busy families needing recurring upkeep.
  • Apartment residents who want bathrooms, kitchens, and floors refreshed.
  • Homeowners preparing for a normal week, guests, or routine maintenance.

Estimated cleaning time

A small apartment may take about 2 to 3 hours. A medium home may take 3 to 5 hours. Larger homes, extra bathrooms, pet hair, heavy kitchen use, or cluttered surfaces can add time. The first visit can take longer because dust and buildup are usually heavier before a recurring rhythm is established.

Room-by-room checklist

The checklist below describes common standard cleaning tasks. Final scope depends on access, time, home condition, and customer priorities.

  • Kitchen: wipe counters, sinks, faucets, tables, backsplashes, and appliance exteriors.
  • Bathrooms: clean mirrors, vanities, sinks, toilets, tubs, showers, fixtures, and floors.
  • Bedrooms: dust accessible furniture, vacuum or mop floors, and refresh visible touch points.
  • Living areas: dust tables, shelves, ledges, and decor; vacuum rugs; mop hard floors.
  • Entryways: remove tracked-in debris, wipe reachable surfaces, and clean floors.

How to prepare

Pick up laundry, papers, toys, and dishes where possible. Secure valuables, note fragile items, and share rooms that should be skipped. Preparation helps more appointment time go toward cleaning rather than moving belongings.

Is standard cleaning enough for a first visit?

Sometimes, but many first visits need deep cleaning if there is visible buildup, heavy dust, or neglected kitchen and bathroom details.

Can I rotate tasks?

Yes. Recurring customers can rotate detail tasks such as baseboards, guest rooms, or extra dusting when time allows.

Are supplies included?

Yes, supplies for typical residential cleaning are provided.
