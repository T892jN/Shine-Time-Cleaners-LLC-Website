Pricing guide

Cleaning prices make more sense when the scope is clear.

Use this pricing guide to understand what affects a residential cleaning estimate before requesting availability.

Request availabilityCompare services

Pricing depends on the work, not just square footage.

Residential cleaning estimates should account for home size, room count, number of bathrooms, flooring, pets, clutter, condition, service frequency, and special instructions. A lightly used 1,500 square foot home can take less time than a smaller apartment with heavy kitchen buildup or a move-out deadline.

Standard cleaning

2-5 hours typical

Best for routine upkeep when surfaces are reachable and buildup is moderate.

Deep cleaning

4-8+ hours typical

Best for first visits, seasonal resets, heavy dust, and detailed kitchen or bathroom needs.

Move-out cleaning

4 hours-full day

Best when a home or apartment is empty or nearly empty and needs turnover cleaning.

What lowers time

Clear counters, picked-up floors, accessible bathrooms, regular maintenance, and a prioritized checklist can reduce the time needed.

What adds time

Heavy buildup, pet hair, many bathrooms, delicate surfaces, cluttered rooms, and appliance interiors can increase time.

How to request an estimate

Include city, home type, approximate square footage, bedrooms, bathrooms, service type, pets, and top priorities.
