FAQ

Residential cleaning questions, answered clearly.

This page is kept for visitors who use the shorter FAQ address.

Request availabilityCompare services

Do you bring cleaning supplies?

Yes. Shine Time Cleaners brings cleaning supplies for typical residential appointments. Customers should mention delicate surfaces, product restrictions, allergies, or preferred products before service.

How long does a cleaning visit take?

Time depends on home size, condition, room count, and service type. A small maintenance visit may take a few hours, while deep cleaning or move-out cleaning can take most of a day.

Can I choose priority rooms?

Yes. Customers can prioritize kitchens, bathrooms, floors, dusting, guest areas, pet-heavy areas, or move-related details so the appointment focuses on the highest-value work first.

Do you clean apartments?

Yes. Apartments, townhomes, condos, and houses can be cleaned when the scope is residential and access details are clear.

Are the testimonials real?

The testimonials shown on this site are clearly marked as placeholder examples until verified customer reviews are available. They are included to show the type of feedback section the site will use.

What is the difference between standard and deep cleaning?

Standard cleaning maintains a home that is already in reasonable condition. Deep cleaning gives more time to buildup, edges, fixtures, baseboards when included, and heavily used rooms.

Do you serve all of Missouri?

Service availability depends on scheduling, travel time, and the exact location. The service areas page explains Missouri cities and nearby communities that may be relevant to service requests.

Can I request only bathrooms and floors?

Yes. Custom priorities can focus on the rooms that matter most, although the estimate should reflect the time and detail required.

Do you provide exact online prices?

The site explains timing and pricing factors because exact pricing depends on home details, condition, and requested scope.

Do you clean interior appliances?

Interior ovens and refrigerators should be requested specifically because they add time and may require special preparation.

How should I prepare for move-out cleaning?

Remove belongings, keep utilities on, share access instructions, and provide any landlord or property manager checklist.

Can cleaning happen while I am away?

Access arrangements should be discussed in advance. Customers should secure valuables, pets, alarms, and entry instructions.

What if I have allergies or product restrictions?

Mention allergies, sensitivities, preferred products, and restricted surfaces before service so the plan can be reviewed.
